# Siemens Business Project Manager Training — Project Overview

## Programme Background
This is a commercial project management training programme hosted by Siemens, focusing on practical business project delivery in industrial and commercial scenarios.
The training introduces the core logic of commercial project management, not only engineering management, but also commercial negotiation, budget control and cross-team coordination.

## My Role
Participant / trainee. I took part in group discussions, simulated project cases and delivered group presentations.

## Learning Objectives
1. Understand the full lifecycle of a commercial project: initiation, planning, execution, monitoring and closure.
2. Learn to identify project risks, manage stakeholders and control project budgets & schedules.
3. Practice cross-team communication and solve conflicts between technical requirements and commercial goals.

## Programme Content Outline
- Project charter & scope definition
- Stakeholder mapping & communication plan
- Risk identification and risk response strategy
- Project budget tracking & milestone management
- Simulation of commercial project negotiation

## Key Deliverables from Me
- Group analysis of simulated business project case
- Personal notes of project management frameworks
- Reflection on team cooperation in simulated project
