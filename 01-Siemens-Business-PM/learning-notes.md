# Learning Notes of Siemens Project Management Training

## Core Concept: Project Full Lifecycle
A complete commercial project has five phases: start, plan, execute, monitor & control, close.
The most easily ignored part is risk monitoring after the project starts.

## Stakeholder Management
Stakeholders include clients, suppliers, team members and managers.
We should classify them by power and interest, then make different communication plans.

## Project Scope Control
Scope creep is a common problem. It means extra requirements keep being added during the project.
We must define clear boundaries at the beginning to prevent endless changes.

## Risk Management
We need to list possible risks, judge their probability and impact, then prepare response plans.
Risks cannot be removed completely; our goal is to reduce loss.

## Personal Takeaway
I used to think project management is just making schedules.
After the training, I realize it focuses more on communication, balance and risk control in real business situations.
